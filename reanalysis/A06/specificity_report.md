# Growth and composition sensitivity

Residual Z scores describe deviations from relationships fitted across this compound collection. E2F response and overall perturbation magnitude are post-treatment molecular proxies, not measured baseline confounders. Residualization can remove real drug biology and does not demonstrate an effect independent of growth. No beneficial target directions, composite geroprotection scores, or composite significance tests are computed.

The ribosome comparison examines one compositional explanation. It cannot establish absolute protein or organelle abundance per cell. Direct cell counts, viability, and total protein per cell are unavailable.
