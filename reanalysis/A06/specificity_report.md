# Growth and composition sensitivity

Residual Z scores describe deviations from relationships fitted across this compound collection. E2F response and overall perturbation magnitude are post-treatment molecular proxies, not measured baseline confounders. Residualization can remove real drug biology and does not demonstrate an effect independent of growth. Separate exploratory indices and their E2F/G2M residual sensitivities are generated by run_prioritization.py and documented in prioritization_report.md. Their target directions are hypotheses, and no composite significance test is computed.

The ribosome comparison examines one compositional explanation. It cannot establish absolute protein or organelle abundance per cell. Direct cell counts, viability, and total protein per cell are unavailable.
