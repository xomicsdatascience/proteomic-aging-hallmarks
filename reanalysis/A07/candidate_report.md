# Interpretation of response examples

Binimetinib and baicalein are retrospective examples of a mitochondrial/inflammatory protein-response combination. They are not predetermined winners of a validated aging screen. Complete mitochondrial and inflammatory annotation members, every displayed sentinel, contradictory results, observation counts, and model intervals are available for all displayed kinase inhibitors. A single MEK inhibitor does not establish a MEK-class effect. Ten micromolar, 24-hour HepG2 responses do not establish clinical exposure or on-target specificity.

DFO remains an assay response control. No compound is relabeled an established geroprotector, pro-aging control, or cytotoxic control from these data alone. A second MEK inhibitor or genetic perturbation, primary-cell dose/viability work, cell-normalized respiration, and compartment-appropriate inflammatory assays would test the proposed mechanism.
